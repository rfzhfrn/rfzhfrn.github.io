About Marvel

The story of the world's most powerful entertainment brand

Marvel Entertainment, LLC is one of the world's most prominent character-based entertainment companies, built on a proven library of over 8,000 characters featured in a variety of media over 85 years. Marvel utilizes its character franchises in entertainment, licensing, publishing, games, and digital media.

  • Founded in 1939 by Martin Goodman as Timely Comics, now a powerhouse in global entertainment.
  • Home to iconic heroes like Iron Man, Spider-Man, Captain America, Thor, and the Avengers.
  • Part of The Walt Disney Company since 2009, expanding across films, TV, games, and comics.

The Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) is the highest-grossing film franchise of all time, with more than $29 billion earned at the global box office across 33 interconnected films and multiple Disney+ series. From the cosmic reaches of space to the streets of New York City, Marvel stories captivate billions of fans worldwide through compelling characters and extraordinary storytelling.
